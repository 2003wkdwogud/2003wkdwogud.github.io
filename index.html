About Me

I am an undergraduate student studying Electrical Engineering at the University at Buffalo. I conduct research in the Media Forensics Lab under Professor Siwei Lyu and serve as the Audio Team Technical Leader.

My research interests include audio deepfake detection, speech processing, and multimedia forensics.

Research

AI-Generated Gunshot Detection

Investigating methods to distinguish real gunshot recordings from AI-generated gunshot sounds.

Group Delay for Audio Forensics

Studying decay-region group delay as a forensic cue for detecting AI-generated impulsive sounds.

Cross-Codec Speech Deepfake Detection

Exploring transfer-aware training data selection to improve detection across unseen neural audio codecs.
