About

I am an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the University at Buffalo and an undergraduate research intern in the Media Forensics Lab (MDFL), advised by Professor Siwei Lyu. I also serve as Audio Team Technical Leader for the Institute for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (IAD) and MDFL.

My research interests lie in speech processing, audio forensics, and multimedia security. In speech processing, I am interested in automatic speech recognition and child speech normalization. In audio forensics, I investigate acoustic cues that distinguish AI-generated sounds from real recordings, including impulsive sound analysis and speech deepfake detection across neural audio codecs.
